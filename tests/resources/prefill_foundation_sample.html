Tar Heel Engineering Scholarship

The Piedmont Education Foundation supports students from Wake County and Durham County who are enrolled full time in an engineering or computer science program.

  • Amount: $2,500
  • Deadline: March 15, 2027
  • State/Territory: North Carolina

How to apply

Applicants must submit a 500-word essay describing their career goals. Two letters of recommendation are required, along with an official transcript. Finalists are invited to an interview in April.

A minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0 is required to apply and to renew the award.

Piedmont Education Foundation, Raleigh, North Carolina. Updated 2026-08-01.
